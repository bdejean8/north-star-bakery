About North Star Bakery

Our Story

North Star Bakery was created to be the kind of neighborhood shop where people can stop in for a dependable loaf, pick up a treat on the way to work, or plan food for a family celebration. Our goal is to make handmade baking feel welcoming and connected to the community we serve.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We choose ingredients with quality, seasonality, and practical local sourcing in mind. When possible, we work with regional suppliers for flour, dairy, fruit, and other staples. We also keep our recipes focused so good ingredients and careful baking remain at the center of each product.

Meet the Team

Lead Baker

Our lead baker oversees daily bread production and keeps our sourdough and seasonal recipes consistent from batch to batch.

Pastry and Cake Team

Our pastry team prepares morning favorites, cookies, and celebration cakes while helping customers choose options for gatherings and special events.

Front Counter Team

Our front counter team welcomes customers, answers ingredient questions, and coordinates pre-order pickups.
