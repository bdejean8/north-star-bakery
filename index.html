Welcome to North Star Bakery

Fresh Baking for Our Neighborhood

North Star Bakery makes breads, pastries, and celebration cakes in small batches. We welcome commuters looking for a quick morning treat, families choosing weekend favorites, and event planners who need something special.

What We're Known For

Our neighborhood favorites include naturally leavened artisan breads, buttery pastries, cookies, and made-to-order cakes. We focus on familiar flavors, careful preparation, and friendly service.
